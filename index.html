Under the hood

A transparent, three-stage pipeline.

The same scikit-learn pipeline you trained, served through FastAPI — no black boxes added on top.

01

Text fusion

Title, company profile, description and requirements are merged and vectorised into the 5,000 most informative terms.

02

Metadata signals

Employment type, experience, education, logo, screening questions and remote flag are encoded alongside the text.

03

Risk scoring

A class-balanced logistic regression returns a fraud probability and a final Fake / Genuine verdict.
